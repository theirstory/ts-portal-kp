# Interview-guide question list (list_number = Q<id>). Interviewers paraphrase, reorder and skip these.

## Personal Background and Journey
Q1: What can you tell us about yourself? (e.g., where were you born, what brought you to the area where you practiced, what influenced you to become a doctor, where were you educated?)
Q2: What inspired you to join Kaiser Permanente initially?
Q3: What was your first leadership position? How did that position come about? Was it offered? Did you seek it?
Q4: Can you share your journey to becoming an Executive Medical Director (or comparable senior physician leader) of your Permanente Medical Group?
Q5: If you were among the first in your role — whether by gender, background, or path — what did that milestone mean to you personally and professionally?
Q6: What challenges or dynamics did you encounter as you stepped into senior physician leadership at that time?
Q7: Tell me about your experience stepping into a groundbreaking or historic leadership role, if that applied to you.
Q8: What would you tell your younger self at Kaiser Permanente, knowing what you know now?
## Organizational Values and Culture
Q9: What sets the Permanente Medical Groups apart from other medical groups?
Q10: How has Kaiser Permanente's mission influenced your career?
Q11: What are the core values that guide Kaiser Permanente's operations and culture, and how do you apply those values in your leadership style?
Q12: Can you describe a pivotal moment in the history of Kaiser Permanente that has shaped its current practices?
Q13: If you could talk to Sidney Garfield, what would you want to know from him?
Q14: If you could talk to Henry Kaiser, what would you want to know from him?
Q15: In your opinion, what would Sidney Garfield and Henry Kaiser say about Kaiser Permanente today?
## Leadership and Innovation
Q16: What were the greatest challenges you've experienced as a leader in this organization?
Q17: Of what are you most proud during your tenure?
Q18: How does Kaiser Permanente foster innovation in health care delivery?
Q19: In your time, how did Kaiser Permanente address health equity and access to care in diverse communities?
Q20: What advice would you give to new physicians joining a Permanente Medical Group?
Q21: What leadership qualities are essential for success at Kaiser Permanente?
Q22: Can you share an example of a successful project or program you led at Kaiser Permanente?
Q23: What were the biggest challenges facing health care in your time, and how did Kaiser Permanente address them?
## Patient Care and Clinical Practices
Q24: How does Kaiser Permanente's integrated care model benefit patients and providers?
Q25: Can you share a story where Kaiser Permanente's approach made a significant difference in a patient's life?
Q26: How does Kaiser Permanente support physician well-being and prevent burnout?
Q27: How does Kaiser Permanente ensure that its physicians stay motivated and passionate about their work?
Q28: What are some key initiatives Kaiser Permanente has undertaken to improve patient care?
Q29: How does Kaiser Permanente ensure continuous professional development for its physicians?
Q30: How does Kaiser Permanente's approach to patient care differ from traditional fee-for-service models?
Q31: How did KP keep health care affordable and competitive in your time?
Q32: How does Kaiser Permanente integrate holistic and preventive care into its medical practices?
Q33: How does Kaiser Permanente ensure quality and safety in patient care?
## Community Engagement and Public Health
Q34: Can you discuss Kaiser Permanente's efforts in community health and preventive care?
Q35: How does Kaiser Permanente engage with the local community to improve public health?
## Technology and Innovation
Q36: What role does technology play in enhancing patient care at Kaiser Permanente?
Q37: How has Kaiser Permanente adapted to changes in health care over the years?
Q38: How does Kaiser Permanente support research and development in medical science?
Q39: What strategies does Kaiser Permanente employ to keep up with the latest medical advancements?
Q40: Can you share an example of how Kaiser Permanente has improved patient outcomes through innovation?
Q41: Can you discuss the impact of Kaiser Permanente's electronic health record system on patient care?
## Leadership Development and Mentorship
Q42: Can you describe the mentorship opportunities available for new physicians at Kaiser Permanente?
Q43: What lessons have you learned from your experiences at Kaiser Permanente that you wish to share with new leaders?
Q44: What's the best way for a new physician to become involved in their department and the medical group?
## Future Goals and Vision
Q45: What role do physician leaders play in shaping the future of health care at Kaiser Permanente?
Q46: What are some of the most rewarding aspects of working at Kaiser Permanente?
Q47: Where do you see KP in 10 to 20 years?
Q48: How do you see KP's history affecting future leaders?
